\subsection{Document Purpose}

The purpose of this High-Level Design (HLD) document is to provide engineering students with a structured architectural blueprint and conceptual review of the system, serving as an educational roadmap rather than a shortcut or turnkey solution. Designed to support independent problem-solving, this document establishes clear technical guardrails and highlights key architectural considerations to prevent students from stumbling into common engineering pitfalls that typically arise from a lack of practical industry experience. By defining subsystem boundaries, core data flows, and design rationales upfront, it allows students to focus their efforts on rigorous implementation, debugging, and execution, ultimately empowering them to bring their project to completion with professional engineering.

\subsection{Document Target Audience}

This technical asset serves as a functional master design document intended for B.Tech Final Year project groups, enterprise project developers, and academic evaluators within the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) framework. It establishes immediate visual transparency regarding bill of materials, project implementations, technical risks, and software-to-hardware configuration pipelines.
